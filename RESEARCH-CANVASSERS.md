# Door-to-door companies: who they are, why they buy, how to find them

## Why they're a good fit

Companies that knock doors, leave door hangers or put up yard signs live on
callbacks. The homeowner usually doesn't decide at the door; they look the
company up later and call back. That gives you two easy pitches:

- **Website:** "When people get a knock or a door hanger from y'all, a lot of
  them look you up before calling back, so it helps to have something to point
  them to." A real site also gives their reps something to show at the door.
- **AI receptionist:** the callbacks come in while the crew is out in the field.
  The receptionist answers, takes the details and books the estimate.

(These are sales angles, not measured facts. Don't quote numbers to them.)

## Which industries canvass

Door-to-door selling is concentrated in a handful of industries: cable,
telecommunications, solar, energy, security, landscaping and construction
([Wikipedia: Door-to-door](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Door-to-door)). Pest
control, cleaning, landscaping, home security, roofing, windows and solar are
all called out as industries where door-to-door works
([Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/blog/sales/door-to-door-sales/)).

For you, the best local targets are the owner-run ones (not national brands):

| Niche | Website angle | AI receptionist angle |
|---|---|---|
| Solar installers | Homeowners research before a big purchase | Callbacks from door knocks and yard signs |
| Roofing (esp. after storms) | Need to look legit next to storm chasers | Flood of calls after a storm |
| Roof / window / gutter cleaning | Often only a Facebook page | Crews are on ladders, can't answer |
| Pest control | Recurring service, people compare | Calls for quotes and scheduling |
| Home security / alarms | Trust matters, people look you up | After-hours calls |
| Pressure washing, lawn care | Usually no site at all | Owner is the crew, can't pick up |

## How to find them in the CRM

1. Open **Find clients**, tap **New search**.
2. Under **Door-to-door companies**, tap a niche (Solar, Roofing, Roof
   cleaning, Window cleaning, Pest control, Home security, Pressure washing,
   Gutter cleaning, Lawn care).
3. Location: `Houston, TX` (then try Katy, Sugar Land, Pearland, The Woodlands,
   Cypress, Spring, Humble, Pasadena, League City).
4. **Website leads** tab: leave "No website only" on. **AI phone leads** tab:
   set Min reviews to 20 or more (busier = more missed calls).
5. Tap **Start outreach** and work the list.

Also watch for them in real life: door hangers on your own door, yard signs
in your neighborhood, and trucks with a phone number but no website on them.
Add those by hand with **New lead**.

## Good to know

- Houston requires a city license for people who go door to door selling to
  homes (Code Chapter 38, per
  [cityrulelookup](https://cityrulelookup.com/cities/houston-tx/soliciting);
  check the city's Administration & Regulatory Affairs page before you rely on
  it). That's *their* reps knocking on homes. You walking into a business's
  storefront to talk to the owner is ordinary business outreach, but respect
  any "No soliciting" sign.
- Their reps get asked "do y'all have a website?" at the door. That's a good
  question to ask the owner: "When your guys knock, what do they point people
  to?"
