# Which side gig? Research and ranking

Researched October 2026. The question: what is easiest to start and fastest to
pay you, ideally **this week**, with little or no money up front?

## The scorecard

| Side gig | Startup cost | First dollar | Realistic month 1–2 | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Websites for local businesses (built with Claude)** | ~$0 | **3–10 days** | $1.5k–$8k (2–10 clients) | **Pick this. It's the main plan.** |
| Facebook Marketplace flipping (start with free items) | $0–$200 | 1–3 days | $200–$600 | Good side cash for week 1. Doesn't scale well. |
| DoorDash / Instacart / Uber | Car + phone | 1–7 days | $15–25/hr | Guaranteed money, but you're trading hours for it. |
| KDP coloring books (AI) | ~$0 | 2–8 weeks | $0–$100 | Flooded. Most books sell nothing. Royalty is $1–3 a book. |
| Faceless YouTube | ~$0–$50/mo | 3–6+ months | $0 | Need 1k subs + 4k hours. Mass-made AI channels get demonetized. |
| Shopify dropshipping | $500–$2,000+ (ads) | 2–8 weeks | Usually negative | Only 5–10% break even. You'd be spending money, not making it. |

## Why websites for local businesses win

1. **You can do the work and I can do the hard part.** Claude builds a
   professional site in minutes (`tools/make_site.py`). Your job is to find
   businesses and talk to them.
2. **One "yes" is worth $500–$1,000+.** One coloring book makes ~$2.80 a sale.
   One dropshipping sale makes a few dollars after ads. Even beginners charge
   $750–$1,500 per site, plus $100–$300/mo for upkeep.
3. **No ad budget and no inventory.** You only spend your time, and a domain
   (~$12/yr) that the client pays for.
4. **It pays fast.** A business owner can say yes and pay a deposit the same day.
   No waiting on algorithms, payouts or Amazon.
5. **It repeats.** Monthly care plans stack up, and happy owners refer other owners.

**The trick that makes it fast: show, don't pitch.** Build the business a free
demo site *before* you contact them. "I already made you a website, want to
see it?" gets far more yeses than "Do you need a website?"

## Honest reality check

- **Most people chasing these gigs make little or nothing.** That's true of
  every gig above, this one included. The money goes to the people who do the
  boring part every day. Here, that's contacting 20–40 businesses a day.
- **$20,000 in 1–2 months is a stretch goal, not a forecast.** It's 20+
  clients at ~$750, or 10–13 at ~$1,500, plus care plans. That's possible with
  heavy daily outreach, but a solid first month is **$1,500–$5,000**.
  See `playbook/money-math.md`.
- **Nobody can promise "instant" money.** The fastest honest path is: demos
  built today, outreach today, first deposit within a week if you put in the
  calls and visits.
