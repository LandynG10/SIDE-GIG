# Cold emails that don't sound like AI

Rules these follow:
- **One specific thing you noticed** about *their* business, never generic.
- **The demo link is the hook.** You already built something, so say so.
- **Value in their terms, conditionally.** "If it brings in one extra job,
  it pays for itself." Never guess their revenue.
- **Short.** 5 short paragraphs max, plain words, contractions, one question
  at the end.
- **Banned:** "I hope this finds you well", "elevate", "unlock",
  "seamless", "boost your online presence", em dashes, lists of 3 adjectives.

Fill in `[brackets]`. Use their first name if you know it, otherwise
"Hi there". Sign with your name and phone number.

## Follow-ups (send as a reply to your first email)

- **Day 2:** "Hey [Name], did you get a chance to look at the preview? Anything you'd change?"
- **Day 5:** "I have room for 2 more launches this week. Want me to hold one for [Business]?"
- **Day 10:** "Last check-in. I'll take the preview down Friday unless you want it. Thanks either way!"

## Sending tips

- Send **20–30 a day max** from a new domain, spread out. Blasting 200 at once
  gets your domain flagged as spam.
- Send from your real name (landyngrant@elgestudio.net), not "info@".
- Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for elgestudio.net (Google Workspace → Apps →
  Gmail → Authenticate email) before sending in volume.
- Include your business address and an easy opt-out line (the CRM adds both).
- **Call or walk in on your best leads.** Email is the lowest-converting
  channel. Use it to reach more people, not instead of calling.
