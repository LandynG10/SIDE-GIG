# The money math: the honest route to $20k

## Typical conversion rates for cold outreach to local businesses

These are rough beginner numbers, not promises:

| Channel | Contacts per sale (approx.) |
| --- | --- |
| Walk-in with demo on your phone | 8–15 |
| Phone call + texted demo link | 15–30 |
| Facebook/Instagram DM with demo | 30–60 |
| Cold email with demo | 50–150 |

## What $20,000 takes

| Average price | Sites needed | At ~1 sale per 20 contacts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $500 | 40 | ~800 contacts |
| $750 | 27 | ~540 contacts |
| $1,000 | 20 | ~400 contacts |
| $1,500 | 14 | ~280 contacts |

Over 8 weeks, ~500 contacts is **about 10 contacts per day, 6 days a week**,
mostly walk-ins and calls. That's real work: about 2–3 hours a day.

Care plans add on top. 15 clients at $79/mo is $1,185/mo of recurring income
that keeps coming after month 2.

## Realistic targets

| Week | Target |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 demos built, 50 contacts, **1–2 deposits** |
| 2 | 60 more contacts, 2–3 sales, first testimonial |
| 3–4 | Raise price to $750. 3–4 sales/week if you keep the contact volume |
| 5–8 | Ask every client for referrals. Raise to $1,000+. Add care plans |

**Good month 1:** $1,500–$5,000. **Strong month 2:** $5,000–$12,000.
Hitting $20k in 2 months means beating these numbers. It's possible if you
do the outreach every single day, but plan for the realistic range.
