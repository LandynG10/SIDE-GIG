# The offer, pricing, and getting paid

## Packages

| Package | Price | What they get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** (first 3 clients) | $500 one-time | One-page site, their domain connected, click-to-call, map, reviews, live in 48h |
| **Standard** | $750–$1,000 one-time | Starter + up to 4 pages (services, about, gallery, contact form) + Google Business Profile cleanup |
| **Care plan** (add-on) | $49–$99/month | Hosting, small edits (2/month), keeping hours and photos current |

Start at $500 for your first 3 clients so you get easy yeses and real
testimonials. Raise the price after every 3 sales.

## Getting paid

- **50% deposit up front, 50% when it goes live.** Never build past the demo
  without the deposit.
- Take payment with a **Stripe Payment Link**, **Square Invoice**, or
  **PayPal/Venmo Business**. All free to set up; they keep about 3% per payment.
  Cash App or Zelle are fine for locals, but Stripe/Square invoices look more
  professional and give you a record.
- Have the client buy the domain in **their own name** (Cloudflare, Porkbun or
  Namecheap, ~$10–15/yr) or buy it for them and add the cost to the invoice.
  Their name on the domain builds trust.
- Send a one-paragraph agreement by text or email before you start (template
  below) and get a "Yes, agreed" back.

### Simple agreement (paste into text or email)

> [Your name] will build and launch a website for [Business] at [domain] for
> $[total]. $[deposit] is due today; the remaining $[balance] is due when the
> site is live. Includes up to 2 rounds of changes. Optional care plan:
> $[x]/month, cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. Reply "Agreed" to confirm.

## Hosting (free)

The site is one HTML file. Free hosting options:
- **Netlify:** drag the `sites/out/<slug>` folder into Netlify, then
  "Domain settings → Add domain".
- **Cloudflare Pages:** same idea, plus a fast global network.
- **GitHub Pages:** works too, but it's more setup.

## Taxes and legal (keep it simple)

- Keep every payment receipt. In the US, side income is taxable, and above
  $400 net in a year you owe self-employment tax. Set aside ~25% of every
  payment.
- You can start as a sole proprietor under your own name. Look into a DBA or
  LLC once money is coming in regularly.
- Only use **real** reviews and photos the business owns or approves. Never
  invent reviews.
- This is not legal or tax advice. Check your local rules.
