# Outreach scripts

Use these word for word at first. Change them once you know what works.
Replace `[brackets]`. Always lead with the demo link.

## Who to target

Look for businesses that have **good Google reviews (4.3★+, 20+ reviews) but
no website**, or one that's clearly broken or ancient. On their Google Maps
listing, the "Website" button is missing or goes to a Facebook page.

Best niches: plumbers, HVAC, roofers, landscapers, pressure washing, auto
repair and detailing, cleaning services, barbers and salons, nail shops, small
restaurants and food trucks, tattoo shops, handymen, movers, pet groomers.
These trades get real money from calls, so a site that makes the phone ring is
an easy sell.

How to find them: open Google Maps, search "[niche] near [your city]", and
click through the listings. Log every one in `tracker/leads.csv`.

## 1. Walk-in (highest close rate)

> Hey, is the owner around? … Hi, I'm [Name], I'm local and I build websites
> for businesses around [city]. I noticed you've got great reviews on Google,
> [X] stars, but no website, so people searching for "[niche] near me" are
> finding your competitors instead. I actually went ahead and built you one
> already. Can I show you? Takes 30 seconds.
>
> *(hand them your phone with the demo open)*
>
> It's got your reviews, click-to-call, your hours, the map. If you like it,
> I can have it live on your own domain by [day] for $[price]. If you don't
> want it, no hard feelings, I'll take it down.

Then **stop talking** and let them scroll.

## 2. Phone call

> Hi, is this the owner of [Business]? My name is [Name], I'm a local web
> designer in [city]. Quick reason for the call: I saw your Google reviews,
> really impressive, and noticed you don't have a website. I built a free
> preview of one for you. Can I text you the link so you can look at it on
> your phone?

If yes, text it right away (see #3). Then: "I'll call you back tomorrow to hear
what you think. What time works?"

## 3. Text / DM (Facebook, Instagram, or after the call)

> Hi [Owner name], it's [Name]. Here's the website I built for [Business]:
> [demo link]
>
> It's set up so customers can call you with one tap from Google. If you want
> it live on your own domain, it's $[price] one-time and I handle everything.
> Want me to make any changes first?

## 4. Email (lowest response rate, but easy to send in bulk)

**Subject:** I made a website for [Business]

> Hi [Owner name],
>
> I'm a local web designer in [city]. I noticed [Business] has [X] great
> Google reviews but no website, so I built one for you to look at:
>
> [demo link]
>
> If you'd like it live on your own domain (like [business].com), it's
> $[price] one-time, and I handle setup and hosting. Happy to make any changes.
>
> Either way, keep up the great work.
>
> [Name] · [phone]

Stay within CAN-SPAM: use your real name and contact info, and stop emailing
anyone who says no.

## 5. Follow-ups (most deals close here)

- **Day 2:** "Hey [name], did you get a chance to look at the site? Anything you'd change?"
- **Day 5:** "Quick one: I've got room for 2 more sites this week. Want me to save a spot for [Business]?"
- **Day 10:** "Last check-in! I'll take the preview down Friday unless you want it. Thanks either way."

## Objections

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "I get enough work from word of mouth." | "That's awesome, it's why your reviews are so good. The site just catches people who Google you after hearing your name, so they don't call your competitor by accident." |
| "I have a Facebook page." | "Keep it! A lot of people don't use Facebook or won't log in. This shows up on Google and lets them call you in one tap." |
| "Too expensive." | "Totally fair. I can do $[lower] if you pay today, or split it into two payments." *(Floor: $300.)* |
| "My nephew is building one." | "Nice! If it doesn't happen, my offer stands, and the preview is yours to look at." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What's the one thing you'd want to see different?" *(Then fix it and send the new version.)* |
| "Is this a scam?" | "Fair question. I'm local, here's my number, you don't pay anything until you've seen it and you're happy. You can even pay half now, half when it's live." |
